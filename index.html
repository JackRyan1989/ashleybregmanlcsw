Ashley Bregman, LSCW

Feel more grounded, connected, and resilient

I ask hard questions with care, guiding you to uncover patterns and challenge deep-rooted beliefs that no longer serve you. Together, we'll work to deepen understanding and foster compassion for all aspects of yourself—even those you find most daunting.

Through this work, you'll cultivate a more courageous and peaceful connection with yourself and in your relationships.

Areas of Focus

  • depression
  • anxiety
  • relational challenges & attachment
  • new parenthood & parents of neurodivergent children
  • neurodiversity-affirming care

These represent just a part of my practice; if you feel we might work well together, I invite you to reach out.

Among my practice specializations, I bring a focus to perinatal mental health, offering care from pregnancy through postpartum and beyond. This time of transition can be both joyous and complex, with powerful shifts in identity. Additionally, I offer guidance to parents navigating the unique and often misunderstood journey of raising neurodivergent children.

Clinical Approach

If this is your thing--I am trained in relational psychodynamic and dialectical behavior therapy (DBT) modalities. I have completed specialty training in perinatal mental health, and have familiarity with internal family systems and sensorimotor approaches. I have additional training in neurodiversity-affirming care.

Biography

I received my master's in social work from Bryn Mawr College. My therapy experience spans community behavioral health, college counseling, and private practice settings. Prior to becoming a therapist, I studied the humanities and worked in the higher education and nonprofit worlds. In my experience, there is so much in common between the study of art of all kinds and exploring the human experience. I am also a parent to two young children, one of whom is neurodivergent.
